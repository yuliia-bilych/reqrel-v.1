The UAV shall fully charge in less than 3 hours.
Charging the drone to full capacity is allowed to take up to 5 hours.
The VehicleCore shall support virtual UAVs.
The VehicleCore shall support up to three virtual UAVs.
The UAV shall be able to fly above 700 feet.
The drone's altitude must be limited to a maximum of 400 feet.
The UAV shall periodically send the Pilot the power and estimated flight time remaining.
At regular intervals the drone must report its remaining power and expected flight time to the pilot.
